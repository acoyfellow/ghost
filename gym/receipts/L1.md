# L1 receipt: gym page generator

## What was built
- `gym/generator/families.ts`: `render(family, seed, salt)` returns `{ html, truth }`. Truth is a keyed hash of (family, seed, salt); F1 and F5 are always held. Layout, colours, fonts, text, target box and overlay box come from a seeded PRNG. Every page sets `window.__ghostReady` (true after 100 to 1300 ms), `window.__ghostAct`, `window.__ghostTruth`, and has exactly one `data-ghost-target`. Nothing external is loaded.
- `site/src/index.ts`: Worker. `GET /gym` lists families and seeds. `GET /gym/<F>/<0..49>` serves the page with `x-ghost-truth` and `cache-control: no-store`. Everything else goes to the `ASSETS` binding.
- `site/wrangler.jsonc`: custom domain `ghost.customer0.dev`, account `29db7c74093386544fed8046ef083a4b`, `workers_dev: false`, assets binding `ASSETS` with `run_worker_first: ["/gym*"]`. No other bindings.
- Salt: `openssl rand -hex 16` in `gym/.salt` (git-ignored) and Worker secret `GYM_SALT`.

## What each family renders
- F1 clean: filler text and a button. Nothing covers it.
- F2 modal: fixed opaque modal. Broken: over the target. Held: placed away from it.
- F3 late-overlay: the modal is hidden until `__ghostAct()`, then appears 20 to 120 ms later, over the target (broken) or away from it (held).
- F4 invisible-cover: transparent absolute div over the target. Held: `pointer-events:none`.
- F5 looks-blocked: visible translucent fixed overlay over the target with `pointer-events:none`.
- F6 canvas-modal: unpositioned-z canvas over the target with a modal drawn on it. Held: canvas `pointer-events:none`.
- F7 shadow-iframe: srcdoc iframe or open shadow root over the target. Broken: inner content covers the target point. Held: host `pointer-events:none`, and the inner content may sit away from the target.
- F8 offscreen-lock: html/body `overflow:hidden`. Broken: target centre at y 900 to 1500, outside the 800 px viewport. Held: inside it.
- F9 moving-target: after `__ghostAct()` the target moves. Broken: by more than its width, so the click point leaves the box. Held: by less than half its size, so the point stays inside.
- F10 disabled-or-inert: broken: target in an `inert` div or a `disabled` fieldset. Held: inert or disabled decoy on an unrelated sibling, `aria-disabled` on a live target, or a `disabled` class on a live target.
